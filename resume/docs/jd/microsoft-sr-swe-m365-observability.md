# Microsoft — Senior Software Engineer, M365（Observability & Intelligent Cloud）

来源：Microsoft 官网（apply.careers.microsoft.com，pid 1970393557041467），China, Beijing（+2 more）。抓取日期 2026-11-01，发布约 12 天前。

## Overview
Microsoft's Observability & Intelligent Cloud team is reimagining how large-scale cloud services stay reliable in the AI era: intelligent platforms that help prevent incidents, detect failures earlier, accelerate diagnosis and mitigation, and reduce operational effort across Microsoft services. The work spans the full reliability lifecycle — telemetry and anomaly detection, AI-powered diagnosis, intelligent automation and operational experiences — and embeds AI into everyday engineering and operations workflows.

## Responsibilities
- Own the design and delivery of major platform capabilities across observability, detection, AI-powered diagnosis and mitigation, operational experiences, and AI evaluation.
- Lead architecture and implementation of scalable, secure, observable, maintainable services supporting Microsoft-scale live-site operations.
- Build AI-powered diagnosis capabilities that reason across telemetry, incidents, service knowledge and operational context to accelerate root-cause identification and guide mitigation.
- Develop intelligent automation for incident investigation, troubleshooting, alert correlation, mitigation and operational decision-making, with appropriate safety and human oversight.
- Drive AI transformation in day-to-day engineering (debugging, incident investigation, development, testing, operational analysis, productivity).
- Convert repetitive operational work and expert knowledge into reusable AI-assisted workflows, agents and automation.
- Collaborate with product, engineering and reliability teams; use incident data, service-health signals and AI evaluation results to prioritize investments.
- Mentor engineers; improve platform adoption through reusable frameworks, APIs, playbooks and self-service.
- Help shape long-term strategy for AI-powered operations, agentic diagnosis, closed-loop service protection and safe automation.

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science or related technical field AND 4+ years technical engineering experience with coding in C, C++, C#, Java, JavaScript, or Python, OR equivalent experience.
- Microsoft Cloud Background Check.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Master's in CS or related AND 6+ years, OR Bachelor's AND 8+ years, OR equivalent experience.
- Designing, building and operating distributed systems, cloud services or large-scale backend platforms.
- Technical depth in service architecture, reliability, debugging, performance, security and operational excellence.
- Applying AI, ML, LLMs, agents or AI-assisted automation to engineering or operational problems (deep ML research not required).
- Using AI effectively in everyday engineering workflows (development, debugging, troubleshooting, data analysis, incident investigation, automation).
- Reasoning across signals, telemetry, logs, dependencies and operational context to diagnose problems.
- Leading ambiguous technical work from framing through architecture, implementation, deployment, evaluation and production operation.
- Cross-team collaboration and communication; growth mindset toward AI transformation.
